Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a fee for slot booking assistance when official portal charges may differ? +
While standard official portal fees are minimal or free, appointment slots often fill up within seconds as soon as the RTO releases them. You are retaining our service desk to continuously monitor portal availability, capture newly released dates instantly, and ensure you secure an appointment without missing critical deadlines or waiting for months.
Can I complete the online application process myself? +
You certainly can! However, if you find that no appointment dates are currently available or if you wish to avoid complex documentation steps, our team can add your profile to our active monitoring queue and automatically secure the next open slot on your behalf.
How do I pay official government portal fees? +
Once our desk completes your portal data entry, we generate your official Application Number and email you the direct official portal link. You make the government fee payment directly to the authority using UPI, Credit/Debit card, or Net Banking.
How will I know my application status? +
You can click the Status Tracker button in our top menu bar at any time to view your real-time processing stage on our tracker portal using your Order ID and Password.
Are you an official RTO or Government Office? +
No. ASK Consultancy is an independent third-party documentation assistance desk. We provide formatting, data processing, and scheduling support to assist individuals with online portal submissions.
